How it works

From chats to litres in three steps

Connect your chats

Drop in the data export from Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, or install the extension to count new chats as you go.

Count the tokens

DripStat counts the tokens in every prompt and reply, day by day, at about four characters per token.

Estimate the water

Tokens become energy, and energy becomes water: cooling at the data center plus the water used to generate the power.

Connect

Two ways to bring your chats in

None of these services let other sites sign in and read your history, so DripStat works from what's already in your hands.

Stays up to date

DripStat Live extension

A browser extension for Chrome, Edge and Brave that counts each new prompt and reply on claude.ai, chatgpt.com and gemini.google.com.

  • Updates DripStat while you chat, no exports needed
  • Keeps only daily token counts, never your messages
  • Skips chats already on the page, so nothing counts twice
Download the extension
Install in Chrome, Edge or Brave
  1. Unzip dripstat-live.zip.
  2. Go to chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped dripstat-live folder, the one with manifest.json inside.
  4. Open DripStat. It says DripStat Live is on when connected.
Your full history

Data exports

Each service will email you a .zip of your chat history. Drop it on DripStat and it works out which service it came from.

  • Claude: Settings → Privacy → Export data
  • ChatGPT: Settings → Data controls → Export data
  • Gemini: Google Takeout → My Activity → Gemini Apps
Import an export

Use both: import once for your history, then let the extension keep it current.

Method

Estimates you can check

Providers don't publish water use per person, so DripStat shows its working. Choose a method, or set the numbers yourself.

Provider-reported
0.44mL / 1k tokens

Data center cooling only, from Google's and OpenAI's own per-prompt figures.

Cooling + power plants · default
1.80mL / 1k tokens

Adds the water evaporated at power plants to make the electricity, at the US average of about 3.1 L per kWh.

Li et al. 2023
16.9mL / 1k tokens

The "Making AI Less Thirsty" study on GPT-3-era hardware. Treat it as an upper bound.

water = tokens ÷ 1,000 × Wh per 1k tokens × (on-site L/kWh + PUE × power-plant L/kWh)
Privacy

Your chats never leave your browser

DripStat has no server and no accounts. It's a static page and an optional extension, and both do all their work on your device.

  • Exports are read in place. Files you drop are opened by the page itself and never uploaded.
  • Only counts are kept. DripStat saves tokens and prompts per day, not what you wrote.
  • Delete anytime. One button clears everything, in the page and in the extension.
  • Open source. Read every line on GitHub.

See your number

It takes a minute with an export, or no time at all with the extension. Start with example data if you just want to look around.

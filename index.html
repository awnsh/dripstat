About these numbers

Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini don't offer a way to sign in and read your token usage from another site, so DripStat connects to each account through its data export. Re-import whenever you want to refresh a service; the new file replaces the old one for that service.

  • Tokens are counted from the text in your exported chats at about 4 characters per token. Hidden reasoning, file and image processing, and the conversation history re-read on every turn are not in the export, so real usage is higher.
  • Provider-reported uses Google's August 2025 figure for a median Gemini text prompt (0.24 Wh, 0.26 mL) and OpenAI's similar 0.34 Wh / 0.32 mL per query. It covers cooling at the data center only.
  • Cooling + power adds the water evaporated at power plants to generate the electricity, using the US average of about 3.1 L per kWh.
  • Li et al. 2023 is the "Making AI Less Thirsty" study, which put GPT-3 at roughly 500 mL per 10–50 responses on older hardware. Treat it as an upper bound.

Your totals are saved in this browser only. Clearing site data or opening the page elsewhere starts empty; re-import your exports to rebuild it.
